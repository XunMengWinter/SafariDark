Use SafariDark

  1. 1

    Enable the extension

    Open Safari Settings, turn on SafariDark, then allow it on the websites you want to darken.

  2. 2

    Use the toolbar popup

    Click the SafariDark toolbar icon in Safari to choose Dark, Original, or Auto.

  3. 3

    Tune the current site

    Use the popup to disable one site, skip pages that are already dark, or adjust brightness, contrast, and sepia.

Local by default

  • Darkening runs in Safari on this Mac.
  • Preferences are saved in Safari extension storage.
  • The optional page control stays off until you enable it.
